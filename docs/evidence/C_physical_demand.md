- Year: Document dated 20 December 2024 (EB156/15)

It expects 67 countries to have too few health workers by 2030. Across 122 countries, nearly one in four doctors is over 55; in high-income countries the figure is nearly one in three. - Year: 18–19 September 2026

### 1.2 Energy and grid

**1.2.1 IEA World Energy Employment 2025**
- Claim: Global energy employment was 76 million in 2024, more than 5 million above 2019. It grew 2.2%, which the IEA calls "nearly double" the rate of overall employment growth, and is expected to slow to 1.3% in 2025. More than half of the roughly 700 energy firms, unions and training institutions surveyed reported "critical hiring bottlenecks." In advanced economies there are 2.4 energy workers near retirement for every new entrant under 25; the ratio is 1.7:1 in nuclear and 1.4:1 in grid roles. New qualified entrants would need to rise 40% by 2030 to stop the gap widening, at an extra USD 2.6 billion a year. The roles in shortest supply are electricians, pipefitters, line workers, plant operators and nuclear engineers.
- Year: Published 5 December 2025 (date per Creamer Media)

## Part 2. Transition pathways (official or near-official pages)

### 2.1 Clinical research
- **SOCRA CCRP.** Category 1 requires two years of full-time work as a clinical research professional within the past five years, with no education documentation needed. Category 2 requires a degree in clinical research plus one year of full-time work (or 1,750 part-time hours) within the past two years. Category 3 requires either a clinical research certificate of at least 12 semester credits or an associate's or bachelor's degree in a science or health field, plus the same one year. https://www.socra.org/certification/ccrp-certification-exam/candidate-eligibility (Primary). Fee not listed on that page.
- **ACRP CCRC.** Requires 3,000 hours of verifiable clinical research work. A waiver of 1,500 hours is available for an active ACRP certification or an approved education program. Internships do not count. Fees: $435 (member, early bird) to $600 (non-member, regular). https://acrpnet.org/certification/crc-certification (Primary)
- **ACRP CCRA.** Requires 3,000 hours of paid human-subject research, with up to 1,500 hours waivable. https://casrai.org/compare/ccra-vs-ccrc-certification (Tertiary; the ACRP CCRA page was not fetched)
- Implication: Every route requires 1–2 years of on-the-job clinical research before certification. A displaced worker enters first as an uncertified coordinator or assistant.

### 2.2 Healthcare administration
- **AHIMA RHIA.** Requires completion of a CAHIIM-accredited bachelor's or master's program in health information management, or a CAHIIM-approved post-baccalaureate HIM certificate. Health informatics degrees do not qualify. Exam fee: $229 (member) or $299 (non-member). https://ahima.org/certification/RHIA (Primary)
- **Graduate certificate example.** Iowa State is cited in 2.4; for health administration, the University of Colorado Colorado Springs (a public university) offers a 12-credit Graduate Certificate in Health Care Administration. Duration and cost are not stated on the page. https://johnsonbethel.uccs.edu/health-care-administration (Primary)

### 2.3 Supply chain
- **ASCM CSCP.** Requires at least one of: three years of related business experience, a bachelor's degree, or a listed certification (CPIM, CLTD, CPSM, CPM and others). Exam fee: $1,295 (CORE member or non-member) or $995 (PLUS member). https://om.utdallas.edu/program-resources/certification-training-prep-courses/cscp ; https://share.ansi.org/wc/Shared%20Documents/Workcred-Reports/Understanding-Certifications-Study/Certification-Overviews/Association-for-Supply-Chain-Management-Certified-Supply-Chain-Professional.pdf (Secondary/Workcred)
- **ASCM CPIM.** No formal prerequisites beyond age 18 and a high school diploma (per an ASCM chapter). A typical university prep course (University of Tennessee at Chattanooga) runs 16 weeks and 48 class hours, with about 4 hours a week of outside study, and recommends sitting the exam 90–120 days after the course. Course fee: $2,899, including an exam voucher and one retake. https://sierrapacific.ascm.org/CPIM_Credential ; https://www.utc.edu/academic-affairs/center-for-professional-education/professional-development/supply-chain/supply-chain-certification-review-courses/cpim/faqs
- **ISM CPSM.** Requires three years of full-time supply management experience plus a bachelor's degree, or five years without a degree. https://www.ismworld.org/advance-with-supply-management-certification/ (Primary)

### 2.4 Project management into construction and infrastructure
- **PMI PMP.** With a four-year degree: 36 months leading projects. Without one: 60 months. Both routes also require 35 contact hours of project management education or the CAPM. Exam fee: $405 (member) or $555 (non-member). https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/pmp-certification-and-exam:-cost-breakdown (Secondary; pmi.org was blocked by the allowlist)
- **CMAA CCM.** Requires 48 months of responsible-in-charge (RIC) experience with a four-year AEC degree; 48 months plus 4 years of general experience with a two-year AEC degree; 72 months with a four-year non-AEC degree; or 48 months plus 8 years of general experience with no degree. Fees: application $350 (member) or $450 (non-member), plus $300 exam. Fees from https://www.cmaanet.org/certification/certified-construction-manager/becoming-ccm (Primary). Eligibility from https://www.irecruit.co/insights/ccm-certification-explained-cmaa-credential-exam-who-benefits (Secondary; the CMAA chart is an image).
- Implication: A PMP holder from tech does not qualify for the CCM directly. A four-year non-AEC degree requires six years of construction RIC experience.

### 2.5 Energy
- **NABCEP PV Installation Professional (PVIP).** Requires 58 hours of advanced training (40 from an accredited provider), OSHA 10-hour Construction, and 6 project credits from completed, permitted and inspected installations in a decision-making role. https://nabcep.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/3.PVIP-B.E-updated-2024.pdf (Primary)
- **Graduate certificate in power systems (Iowa State University).** 12 credits, "1 year+," $900 per credit (about $10,800), bachelor's degree with a 3.0 GPA required. https://iowastateonline.iastate.edu/programs-and-courses/certificate-programs/power-systems-engineering-graduate-certificate/ (Primary)
- **Utility rotational programs.** FirstEnergy's Rotational Engineering Program (June 2026 intake) runs two years as typically four six-month rotations. It requires an ABET-accredited electrical, civil or mechanical engineering degree and pays $70,000–$80,000, or $85,000–$95,000 in New Jersey. https://www.builtinchicago.org/job/engineer-ii-rotational-engineering-program-june-2026/9932326. CHA Consulting's utilities rotation covers gas, distribution, transmission and substations, requires a bachelor's in engineering, and pays $68,000–$80,000. https://jobs-challp.icims.com/jobs/7398/entry-level-utilities-engineer---rotation-program/job. Both are job postings (Primary for the employer). Both require an engineering degree, so they are not open to non-engineers.
- **IEEE PES.** See 1.2.5. IEEE PES is a source of demand evidence, not a credential pathway.

### 2.6 Manufacturing
- **ASQ CQE.** Requires 8 years of on-the-job experience in the body of knowledge, 3 of them in a decision-making role. Degree waivers: bachelor's −4 years, master's or doctorate −5 years, associate's −2 years, trade school −1 year. Exam fee: $550, with $100 off for members. https://www.asq.org/cert/quality-engineer (Primary)
- **ASQ CSSBB.** Requires either two completed Six Sigma projects with affidavits, or three years of relevant full-time work plus one project. Fee: $485 (member) or $585 (non-member), as of September 2026. https://umbrex.com/resources/professional-credentials-directory/cssbb/ (Tertiary; consistent with the ASQ affidavit form https://asq.org/cert/resource/docs/ssbb_affidavit.pdf)
- **SME CMfgT.** A minimum of four years of combined manufacturing-related education and/or work experience is recommended. Fee: $490 (non-member) or $245 (member). https://www.sme.org/cmfgt (Primary)
- **A3 Certified Robot Integrator.** Confirmed as a company-level certification. It involves pre-qualification, an on-site audit, a practical assessment of key personnel, a robot safety risk assessment and an exam. It is not an individual credential. https://www.automate.org/robotics/robotic-integrator-certification-program/become-a-certified-robot-integrator (Primary)

### 2.7 Education (alternative certification)
- **US example: Texas State University alternative teacher certification.** Requires a bachelor's degree in any field with a 2.75 GPA (conditional entry below that). The program comprises 9 credit hours online, 50 field hours, then either a semester of clinical teaching or a year-long paid internship as Teacher of Record. Tuition: $4,400 for 15 credit hours. https://www.education.txst.edu/ci/programs/alt-teacher-cert.html (Primary)
- **Australia: High Achieving Teachers Program (HAT@ECU, Edith Cowan University).** An 18-month accelerated Master of Teaching for non-teaching graduates with a WAM of 70 or more. It includes $18,000 in scholarships ($15,000 in year 1 and $3,000 in year 2; the page is internally inconsistent). Participants are paid while studying: 0.4 FTE in year 1, 0.6–0.8 FTE in year 2, then full time. Total: up to 160 days of paid employment. https://www.ecu.edu.au/degrees/high-achieving-teachers-program (Primary)
- **Teach For Australia Leadership Development Program.** Runs two years (optionally three). Associates teach at 0.8 FTE on a base salary of about $63,000–$77,000 while completing a Master of Teaching (Secondary) (Leading Learning) with ACU. The participant contributes $12,200; the rest is covered by scholarship. Requires a non-teaching bachelor's degree and Australian citizenship or permanent residency. https://teachforaustralia.org/leadership-development-program/ (Primary)

### 2.8 Regulatory and compliance
- **RAPS RAC.** Requires a bachelor's degree plus 3 years of regulatory or regulatory-related experience, a master's plus 2 years, a doctorate plus 1 year, or 10 years with no degree. Exam fee (2027 pricing): $620 (member) or $780 (non-member). https://www.raps.org/rac/rac-eligibility (Primary)

## Caveats and contradicting evidence

1. **Shortages concentrate in licensed or in-person roles.** EURES's top shortages (welders, nurses, cooks, electricians), the IEA's (electricians, pipefitters, line workers, plant operators) and ABC's are trades or clinical roles. White-collar professionals displaced from digital work cannot fill them without multi-year retraining and, often, a licence. The white-collar share of physical-economy demand is not separately quantified in any source found.
2. **Experience gates.** Most of the professional credentials above require 1 to 8 years of in-sector experience before certification: CCRP 1–2 years, CCRC 3,000 hours, CPSM 3–5 years, PMP 3–5 years, CCM 4–6+ years of construction RIC, CQE 8 years less waivers, RAC 1–10 years. Credentials therefore validate a transition rather than enable it, and entry happens at a junior level, often with a wage cut.
3. **Degree barriers.** The RHIA requires a CAHIIM-accredited HIM program. Utility rotational programs require ABET engineering degrees. The power systems certificate requires an engineering bachelor's degree in practice.
4. **Demand is cyclical and policy-sensitive.** US energy jobs fell 1% in 2025 (USEER 2026). ABC's estimate fell from 439,000 (2025) to 349,000 (2026). JSA's shortage rate fell from 33% to 29%. LBNL queue volumes fell 12% in 2024 and 10% in 2025.
5. **Projections are not vacancies.** Deloitte/MI's 3.8 million is gross openings over 10 years, including retirement replacement. The 1.9 million "unfilled" figure is a scenario. WHO's 11.1 million is a needs-based gap measured against UHC benchmarks, not funded posts. Most of the health and teacher gaps fall in low- and lower-middle-income countries (sub-Saharan Africa needs 15 million of the 44 million teachers), where AI-exposed white-collar workers are less concentrated.
6. **Geographic mismatch.** Shortages in nursing, teaching and construction are often regional, rural or in other countries, while displaced digital workers cluster in metros. SoWN 2025 reports that 78% of nurses are in countries holding 49% of the population.
7. **Wages.** Energy pays a premium (median $63,000, 24% above the national median), but teaching salaries (TFA $63,000–$77,000 AUD) and entry-level clinical research or administration pay may sit below mid-career tech pay, which reduces take-up.
8. **The 60% IEA figure is not supported by the IEA's own release.** Use "more than half."
9. **Ageing evidence is dated or partial.** The manufacturing figure (25% over 55) is from 2019. CEWD's latest public figures describe tenure, not retirement eligibility.
10. **Consultancy-sourced ranges.** The IEEE PES estimate of 450,000–1.5 million power engineers comes from Kearney and spans more than threefold.
